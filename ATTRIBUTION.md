# Content catalog attribution

The catalog snapshots in `config/content_catalog` are derived from the Apache-2.0-licensed
[Project NOMAD](https://github.com/Crosstalk-Solutions/project-nomad) collection manifests.
They retain the upstream public download URLs for Kiwix ZIM archives and map packages.

Ember Vault is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Project NOMAD
or Crosstalk Solutions. Individual downloaded works remain subject to their respective licenses.

The offline map renderer bundles MapLibre GL JS and the PMTiles JavaScript library under their
respective open-source licenses. Offline map font glyphs are derived from Noto Sans, licensed
under the SIL Open Font License. Map content remains attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors.
